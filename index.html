About

This website serves as a baseball dashboard which aims to provide all the now-standard advanced stats (bat speed, xwOBA, etc.) while also foregrounding some newer, more exciting stats (e.g. ideal attack angle).

Why this site?

A lot of other baseball sites and dashboards can get pretty kitchen-sink with the info they throw you. The goal of this site is to serve both the essential and the esoteric to users in a modular way, such that they don't have to cross-reference fifteen different custom-made tables.

What unique features does it offer?

To my knowledge, few sites offer as extensive an array of rolling stats as mine does. It also has useful radial charts that let us compare a player's current performance against both other players and his past self.

Where is the data from?

All data is from MLB's Savant platform.

Who am I?

I'm a data scientist by degree and training, and you can find my sports analytics work at thirdcc.cc.
